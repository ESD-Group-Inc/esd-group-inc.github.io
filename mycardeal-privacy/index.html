Privacy Policy – MyCarDeal: Car Cost Calculator

Effective date: October 13, 2026

MyCarDeal is published by Esd Group Inc., Hamilton, Ontario, Canada.

What we collect

Nothing. MyCarDeal has no account or sign-up, and we do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Your deals

All calculations and saved deals stay only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the unlock has been purchased. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Fonts

The app may download its text fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this reveals your device's IP address to Google. No deal data is sent.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and no information is collected from anyone.

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
